FAQs

Straight Answers, No Guesswork

The calls we get every week, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

What cleaning interval makes sense in Guelph?

Two to four years fits most homes. Campus-area rentals, houses with shedding pets, or anyone beside active construction or farm fields should lean shorter. Your before and after photos from the first cleaning set the honest baseline.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Certified and insured on every visit, without exception. Ontario issues no trade licence for duct cleaning, so certification, training, and insurance are the protections that actually exist, and ours travel with the crew.

How can I confirm the work was really performed?

Photos of your duct interiors, shot before and after on your property and reviewed with you before departure. We replace trust with evidence.

Would a slow dryer be the vent or the machine?

Far more often the vent. Rises and bends in Guelph laundry paths gather lint until airflow chokes. We clear the full path, measure at the termination, and tell you candidly if the appliance itself seems to be the problem.

Describe the cleaning process and its length.

Collection equipment attaches to the system, registers come out for washing, branches are brushed under suction section by section, the blower compartment is cleaned, and photos close it out. Most Guelph homes take three to five hours.

Can the odour at heating season startup be removed?

Yes. Residue in the ducts carries it, cleaning removes the residue, and a sanitizer pass over the freshly cleaned surfaces handles the remainder. Applied in that order, it stays gone.

Our home dusts over constantly. Is it the ducts?

Possibly the leaks rather than the dust. Return-side openings draw cellar and cavity air into circulation, and we can inspect and seal for exactly that before you spend on repeated cleanings.

Do you work outside Guelph city limits?

Yes. Guelph plus Rockwood, Fergus, Acton, Erin, and the Wellington countryside, along with Cambridge and Kitchener. Confirm rural addresses when booking.

Do you serve areas outside of Guelph, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Guelph and nearby communities including Rockwood, Fergus, Acton, Cambridge, and the surrounding area.
